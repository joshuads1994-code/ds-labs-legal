1. Publisher and controller

Publisher brand: DS Labs. Legal data controller: [REQUIRES OWNER'S LEGAL NAME AND BUSINESS CONTACT ADDRESS]. Privacy requests: joshuads1994@gmail.com (subject: Card Reader Pro privacy).

2. App functions and device data

Card Reader Pro scans collectible trading cards using camera access or images selected by the user, recognizes text on the device using ML Kit, looks up card details and prices, and manages an on-device collection, wishlist and recent scan history. The reviewed app does not require user registration or a DS Labs-hosted cloud account.

3. Camera, gallery and searches

Camera access is requested for scanning. Image selection is performed through the Android system picker. In the reviewed code, on-device OCR does not intentionally transmit raw camera frames or chosen gallery photos to DS Labs servers. Recognized card names, card/set numbers, and user search terms may be sent to third-party catalogue services for search results. Other SDK behavior will be verified against the final build.

4. Local collection, backups and exports

The card collection, wishlists, purchase-price notes, conditions and recently scanned cards are stored as files in the app's private device storage. They are not synced to a DS Labs account in the version reviewed. Android device backup or transfer may include some files under the current development settings; the final behavior must be verified and disclosed. Users can intentionally export or share data to other apps; the recipient's policy applies.

5. External providers and links

The app may contact TCGdex, Pokémon TCG API, Scryfall, YGOPRODeck and TCGCSV for card data and prices. Search terms, card numbers and necessary connection information, such as IP addresses, may be processed by these providers. The app may open Cardmarket, CardTrader, TCGplayer or eBay for pricing and product details. These external providers govern their own processing and retention.

6. Advertising and consent

The app is designed to use Google AdMob banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Subject to applicable rules, consent and device settings, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process advertising or app-set identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device or app interactions, diagnostics and other information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and Mobile Ads SDK disclosures. Where required, Google UMP collects or manages advertising consent and the app must provide a privacy options entry point.

7. Legal bases, retention and rights

Data needed for user-requested operations is processed on applicable contractual or legitimate-interest grounds as appropriate; optional advertising data uses consent when legally required. Local data generally remains until deleted by an in-app action, Android app-data clearing, or uninstall, subject to device backup practices. Providers may retain information under their policies. Applicable laws may grant access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability and withdrawal rights. Contact the controller above; you may lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.

8. Children, updates and contact

The intended age groups and any additional protections for minors will be set after the final Google Play audience declaration. This notice will be updated when the final code and partner processing are confirmed. Contact joshuads1994@gmail.com.

2. Funzionalità e dati sul dispositivo

Card Reader Pro permette di scansionare carte collezionabili tramite fotocamera o immagini selezionate, riconoscere testo sul dispositivo con ML Kit, cercare informazioni e prezzi e gestire raccoglitore, desideri e scansioni recenti. 3. Fotocamera, galleria e ricerche

Il permesso fotocamera serve alla scansione. Le immagini possono essere selezionate tramite lo strumento Android. Il codice esaminato non prevede l'invio intenzionale a server DS Labs delle foto o dei fotogrammi della fotocamera per eseguire l'OCR, che opera localmente. Nomi delle carte, numeri di collezione, set e parole cercate possono invece essere inviati a servizi di catalogazione esterni. Raccoglitore, desideri, prezzi di acquisto, condizioni, note e cronologia sono salvati nell'archivio privato dell'app sul telefono. La versione esaminata non sincronizza questi dati con un account DS Labs. Il backup o il trasferimento Android possono includere alcuni file nella configurazione di sviluppo attuale; la versione finale dovrà chiarire questo aspetto. Le esportazioni o condivisioni volontarie trasferiscono i dati all'app destinataria.

L'app può consultare TCGdex, Pokémon TCG API, Scryfall, YGOPRODeck e TCGCSV per schede e prezzi. Tali richieste possono contenere termini di ricerca, codici delle carte e dati tecnici di connessione come l'IP. I collegamenti verso Cardmarket, CardTrader, TCGplayer ed eBay rimandano a servizi che applicano le proprie informative.
