ArrowFlow — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Publisher: DS Labs

This policy explains how the ArrowFlow Android game handles information, including advertising and consent choices. It applies to ArrowFlow, not to other DS Labs apps.

1. Game data

ArrowFlow is designed to run without requiring a user account. Game progress and preferences may be stored locally on your device. The game does not require you to provide your name, email address or a password to play. Local data can generally be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it; device backup settings may affect retention.

2. Advertising and third-party services

ArrowFlow integrates Google advertising technology (Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK), which may process information such as device identifiers (including advertising identifiers when available), IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, diagnostic data and consent signals, subject to your device settings and applicable law. Google and its advertising partners may use data to serve, measure, secure and, where permitted, personalize ads. Rewarded ads are optional: you choose whether to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game benefit. Other ad formats may appear at natural breaks.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob policies and privacy guidance.

3. Consent and your choices

Where required, ArrowFlow uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising privacy choices. You can decline optional purposes where offered, and you may be able to revisit privacy options in the app. You can also manage advertising identifiers and privacy settings in Android. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads; non-personalized or limited ads may still be displayed where legally permitted.

4. Purposes and legal grounds

Local game state supports game functionality. Advertising-related processing supports ad delivery, reward verification, measurement, fraud prevention and security. Depending on the processing activity and jurisdiction, applicable grounds may include consent, legitimate interests or compliance with legal obligations. Google's own policy describes its processing and legal grounds.

5. Sharing and international transfers

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and authorized advertising partners. Their systems may process information outside your country, subject to applicable transfer safeguards. DS Labs does not claim to control third-party retention periods or infrastructure.

6. Retention

Local game state generally remains on the device until removed or overwritten. Advertising-related data is retained under the policies and settings of the relevant third-party providers. See Google's privacy policy for more information.

7. Children

ArrowFlow is not specifically directed to children. Advertising configuration and any applicable age-related requirements must be observed before the game is made publicly available. If you believe a child's personal information has been processed improperly, contact us.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of personal data, withdraw consent, and complain to a supervisory authority. For data handled directly by Google, consult Google's privacy controls. For publisher-related requests, contact DS Labs using the address below.

9. Security and changes

We aim to minimize data handled by the game. No system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy when features, providers or legal requirements change; the effective date will be revised.

10. Contact

DS Labs — contact: joshuads1994@gmail.com. For formal data-protection requests, specify “ArrowFlow privacy” in the subject line.

Publication note: This policy describes the intended ArrowFlow implementation. Before production release, the developer must verify that actual SDKs, permissions, consent flows, age classification and Google Play Data safety declarations match this text.
